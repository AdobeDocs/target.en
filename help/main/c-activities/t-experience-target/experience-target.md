# Get started with [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] {#get-started-xt}

>[!BEGINSHADEBOX]

**On this page:** Learn what **[!UICONTROL Experience Targeting]** is, how it fits into personalization, and how a scenario targets different audiences with different experiences.

>[!ENDSHADEBOX]

[!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] (XT) delivers content to a specific audience based on a set of marketer-defined rules and criteria. It is valuable for defining rules, including [geo-targeting](/help/main/c-target/c-audiences/c-target-rules/geo.md), that target a specific experience or content to a particular audience. Several rules can be defined in a single activity to deliver different content variations to different audiences.

When visitors view your site, [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] evaluates those visitors to determine whether they meet the criteria that you set. If they meet the criteria, they enter the activity, and the experience designed for the qualifying audience displays.

[!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] is a first step into the world of personalization and often begins with A/B testing. A marketer runs an A/B test and, by using reporting audiences, discovers that different experiences resonate with different audiences. [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] lets the marketer easily use that discovery and switch to long-term targeting of content to those different audience segments.

## Scenario: from A/B testing to Experience Targeting {#xt-scenario}

Suppose that your website features outdoor equipment. Your reports for A/B testing indicate that you have three experiences that resonate with different audiences. To apply this discovery, you want to display a different hero image on your homepage to appeal to each audience, depending on the location or hobbies of the visitors. You want to display a hiking image to visitors from New Jersey, a surfing image to visitors from Hawaii, and a third image to an audience that likes bicycling.

An [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] activity lets you target these three audiences with a different hero image for each. The following video describes how to take A/B testing to the next level with [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting]. It shows how to:

* Configure an [!UICONTROL Experience Targeting] activity by using the three-step guided workflow.
* Deliver location-specific content to audiences in different geographic areas, by creating experiences targeted to both states and a third experience targeted to biking enthusiasts.
* Reorder the three experiences to ensure that the right content is delivered to the right audience.
